# Pitch: ZENN

## Core loop
Players control a physics sphere with single joystick (and some swipe gestures) while trying their best to get it through an obstacle course without falling off the level.
Each level has checkpoints that are less then a minute apart, so you can stop a session at any time and complete later.
 
## Meta
In a nutshell: Speedrunning/secrete hunting.
Each completed/partially completed level has a certain value in game points (marbles) that adds up to the player's total point count. The faster you complete a level and the harder it is, the more points you get from it. But attempting a level over and over again with a higher/same time or staying on the same checkpoint wont net more points (Although, reaching secrete spots on certain levels also gives you an additional point bonus, regardless of the time or completion, could be a flat +10, +25... Or a multiplier for the entire level X1.5, X2... This should be a way for more casual players to feel a sense of progression without having to get sucked into the speedrunning aspect of the game right away.)
You hop on, see if you can progress, getting to the next checkpoint, a better time on a specific level or find a new secrete area that might help you get more global points. Clock out. 

## Why come back?
Gaining more global points allows you to buy cosmetics for your ball, silly stuff like giving it arms or a hat, or even changing the base shadier that is used on the ball by default: anything from texture swaps to fresnel effects and cell shading...
Getting faster times puts you on a global leaderboard in addition to more points...
Exploring the physics movement of the game is fun on it's own and allows for players to simply find interesting tech that they didn't know about before.

## Who and when
People that like puzzle games but also the potential for a more engaging challenge. Some are PC gamers who are on the fence about mobile games and some are just people looking for a more "physical"/involved puzzle game.
Played anywhere you would play other puzzle games: train, plane, car ride, doctor's office... Anywhere there's time to kill.

## Goals
Players progress a level one checkpoint at a time, maybe even finish it if they where close at the end, get more points out of a level via the methods listed above, buy new cosmetics using points, and reaches a faster time on the leaderboard. Around 3 minutes a session at most, but more engaged players might stay for longer. Levels are not meant to be completed in one go, they have checkpoints, which players can reach within less then a minute.

## Feel
- Rolling a marble on different surfaces (Wood, glass, metal, stone...)
- It falling on a materials will have it's own thud sound effect.
- Collecting new points from a level will have a the sound of jingling marbles in a sack.
- Clicking a button in a menu will have the sound of two glass marbles hitting each-other.
